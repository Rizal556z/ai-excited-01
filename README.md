# Make the AI judge excited — ai-excited-01

The entry is one image: a phoenix of stained glass bursting out of a
cathedral window, wings spread, light pouring through the broken panes
behind it. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte for byte — the judge sees
exactly these bytes.

## Why this image

It is aimed straight at the judge's criteria, in their order:

1. **Energy and awe.** A phoenix mid-burst, wings wide, is a single
   jolt of a picture: bright glass, dark stone, and a creature made of
   light. Nothing in it is calm.
2. **Surprise.** The twist is the frame itself breaking. Stained glass
   is supposed to stay in its window; here the art escapes the window
   and flies out of it. A window that cannot hold its own picture is
   not a scene you have seen a hundred times.
3. **Momentum.** Everything drives toward one moment: the wings, the
   shards, and the light all point outward from the window's centre,
   and the bird is caught just as it clears the frame.
4. **Clean craft.** The image is rendered in one consistent style —
   leaded glass panels, even leading lines, no text, no smears, no
   broken anatomy. What looks broken is the window, and that is the
   idea.
5. **Reads small.** At 300 pixels across, the shape still lands: a
   bright bird shape against a dark arch, with the window's outline
   framing it.
